Got questions?

Frequently Asked
Questions

Is NeuralPrep completely free?+
Yes! 100% free. No signup required for guest access. All features — AI topics, PDF export, timetable, chatbot, leaderboard — are completely free forever.
How accurate are the score predictions?+
Our Linear Regression model is trained on real academic datasets. It provides reliable estimates based on your mid/assignment/attendance scores. Predictions improve with accurate input data.
Which branches are supported?+
CSE/IT, ECE/EEE, Mechanical, Civil, MBA/MCA, and Custom. The Custom option lets any student from any branch add their own subjects with AI topic suggestions.
How does the Smart Timetable work?+
The timetable auto-generates based on your predicted scores — weak subjects get more study sessions, strong ones get fewer. You can switch between Daily, Weekly or Exam-Deadline views and export as PDF.
Can I use it on my phone?+
Yes! NeuralPrep is fully mobile-responsive and installable as a PWA. On Android: Chrome menu → Add to Home Screen. On iPhone: Safari Share → Add to Home Screen. Works offline too!
